Trade-in mounts: grind vs. buy the mats

Every mount unlocked by trading in items or currency: what you actually need, where to turn it in, whether the trade-in materials (or the mount itself) are marketboardable, and a straight verdict on grinding it out versus buying your way there.

Pilot page: one mount in so far, while the format gets worked out before a full data-gathering pass across every trade-in mount. Not linked from the main list yet.

Every mount

Prices, where shown, are live North America region lowest listings and recent average sale price from the Universalis API.

    No mounts match these filters. Pick "All" to see the full list.

    Sources and how this was built

    • Acquisition details (cost, reputation rank, vendor, tradeability): Console Games Wiki item pages, checked directly rather than taken from Teamcraft.
    • Market prices: lowest current listing and recent average sale price, pulled live from the Universalis API for the North America region, for any trade-in material that turns out to be marketboardable.
    • Item IDs and icons: xivapi/ffxiv-datamining Item.csv, icons served via XIVAPI.
