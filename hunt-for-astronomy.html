Hunt for Astronomy: what's worth the tomes

Every item in the First Hunt for Astronomy trove, where else it drops, what it goes for on the NA market board, and a straight call on whether the event is the right way to get it. Tick what you want and it keeps a running tomestone total.

  • Event: Sep 9 – Oct 19, 2026 (PDT)
  • Mogpendium claims: until patch 8.05
  • Shop exchanges: until patch 8.1
  • Prices captured: Sep 17, 2026

    The short version

    Most of this shop is stuff you can get elsewhere. The event matters for one mount, a couple of oddballs, and a few cheap items that sell for real gil.

    FOMO check

    • The uolon horn comes back. The official page says it returns in the Second Hunt for Astronomy, and tokens from this hunt carry over. Teamcraft's data also shows a second-hunt listing that sells tokens for 30 Astronomy II tomes, so missing a minimog week isn't fatal (datamined, could change).
    • MGP card, both special maps and the job mastery prisms are trove regulars. They're also stocked in the "previous" and "past" irregular tomestone exchanges, so there's no rush.
    • Genuinely trove-only right now: the uolon horn (until the Second Hunt) and the Mameshiba Neckerchief. Everything else, including the Ananta and Kotokaze cards, has a permanent Triple Triad or NPC source.
    • Tradeable trove items get flooded. Dhalmel whistles and emerald bardings are already listing well under their recent median. If you're buying to sell, sell early.

      Sources and how this was built

      • Reward list and costs: official Lodestone event page.
      • Item IDs and tradeability: xivapi/ffxiv-datamining Item.csv.
      • Alternate sources (shops, drops, FATEs, crafts, ventures): FFXIV Teamcraft extracts, linked per item. Teamcraft's drop data can be incomplete, so an item with "no other source" is worth a second look on Garland Tools.
      • Market prices: lowest current listing and recent average sale price, pulled live from the Universalis API for the North America region. Also browsable on Ultros. Items marked "not captured" have live links only.
      • Tomestone payouts per objective aren't listed on the event page; check the Mogpendium in game for the exact numbers.
